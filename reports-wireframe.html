1 How you navigate, step by step

Your strongest step is Structured. You feel most at home planning ahead — but you flex through all five as situations change.

2 How you tend to decide

You naturally prefer to plan ahead and understand your options before deciding. You feel most at ease with organised systems and time to think. In fast-moving moments, borrowing a little from your Adaptive side may also help.

3 Communication suggestions

  • Share your plan early, so others can weigh in before it feels fixed.
  • Name which decisions you'd like time to think through vs. can make on the spot.
  • When plans change, say so out loud — others may not see your recalculation.

4 Working with different preferences

A partner who leans Adaptive may want to decide more quickly than feels comfortable to you. Agreeing when a decision needs a plan and when it's fine to move fast can help you both.
