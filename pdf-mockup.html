What this means

You tend to make money decisions that create safety and look after the people you love.

With Security and Connection leading your compass, you're drawn to choices that reduce worry and support your family — a steady foundation others rely on.

🛡 When Security leads

You make money decisions that protect you first. Stability and a buffer against the unexpected matter more than chasing the highest return.

❤️ When Connection leads

Your spending and giving naturally centre on family and the people closest to you — that's where money feels most meaningful.

An area to explore

Growth sits quieter on your compass right now. You may wish to explore how a little long-term investing could quietly fund the security and experiences you value most.

Worth reflecting on

  • How much is “enough” safety — before it starts holding other parts of your life back?
  • Are you as generous with your own future as you are with the people around you?
  • When keeping money safe and enjoying it today pull in different directions, which do you tend to follow?

A few practical next steps

  • Name the amount that would let you feel genuinely secure — then decide what the money beyond that line is free to do.
  • Add yourself to the list: set aside a little for your own goals before you give or support, so both can last.
  • Explore one small, automatic long-term investment to quietly fund the security and experiences you value.

How you tend to decide

You naturally prefer to plan ahead and understand your options before deciding. You feel most at ease with organised systems and time to think. In fast-moving moments, borrowing a little from your Adaptive side may also help.

Working with a different preference

Opportunities for Alignment

  • Agree a shared “safety baseline” you both feel good about — then give each of your leading directions room above it.
  • Let James lead on a long-term investing goal, and Sarah lead on how you support family and mark the moments that matter.
  • Name one shared goal that needs both instincts — a trip you save toward (Connection + Security) or a fund you grow together (Growth + Legacy).

Conversations worth having

“What would need to be true for us both to stop worrying about money?”

“How do we balance being generous with looking after ourselves?”

“Where do you hope we are financially in ten years?”

“What's one goal you'd like us to start working toward this year?”

A simple monthly money check-in

  • One thing either of us is worried about
  • One decision to make together
  • One thing we're saving toward — and how it's going
