## Section — FAQ

- **Heading:** Good to know

**Q: Do I need to share my financial details?**
No. Wealth Compass never asks for balances, income, or account access. It's entirely about how you feel and what you value.

**Q: Is it really free?**
Yes — downloading the app, taking the full assessment, seeing your own compass, and the conversation-starter cards are all free, no account required. A one-time $10 unlock adds a second person, overlays your two compasses, and gives you personalised recommendations and an action plan.

**Q: How does comparing with my partner work?**
You each take it on your own, then one of you shares a short passphrase. Entering it brings both compasses together in one private view.

**Q: Is this financial advice?**
No. It's a reflection tool to help you understand your own priorities and have better conversations. It doesn't recommend products or investments.

**Q: Can I take it again?**
Yes — your values shift over time, and it's worth revisiting. Each result stands on its own so you can see what's changed.
